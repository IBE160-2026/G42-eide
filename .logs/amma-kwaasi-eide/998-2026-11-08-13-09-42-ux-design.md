- **UX decisions (all recorded in `.memlog.md`):**
  - **Interface:** Norwegian Bokmål, with English technical documentation. Labels: `Sammendrag`, `Flashkort`, `Quiz`, `Nøkkelbegreper`, `Last opp`, `Generer`, `Detaljnivå` (options `Kort` / `Standard` / `Detaljert`).
  - **Key Flow 1 (Odeibea):** understand, practise and verify, with clickable "PDF-side N" links that open the user's own PDF without storing it on the server.
  - **Key Flow 2 (the examiner):** demo mode, which works offline with no API key.
  - **Layout:** a single page with four tabs. Flashkort are shown one at a time, and Quiz answers are revealed with "Vis svar" (display-only, no scoring). `Ny fil` asks for confirmation; in demo mode the button is `Tilbake til start`, without confirmation.
  - **States, notices and error texts:** complete Norwegian texts for all states and errors. Limits are filled in from `/api/status`.
  - **Accessibility:** the target is WCAG 2.2 AA, not a compliance claim. Floor AF-1 to AF-13, including `aria-disabled`, the standard tabs pattern, a status live region and 320px reflow. Three test methods with pass criteria.
  - **Visual direction:** a refined "Rolig notatbok" (warm off-white, teal `#1D5C63`), light mode only, with approved tokens.
- **Validation:**
  - Rubric walker + accessibility review: 1 critical, 7 high, 19 medium and 20 low findings.
  - The targeted re-check resolved the critical finding and all 7 high findings.
  - The final consistency check passed.
- **Finalisation:** `DESIGN.md` and `EXPERIENCE.md` are set to `status: final`. The approved mockup was moved to `mockups/`; the explored alternatives stay in `.working/`.
