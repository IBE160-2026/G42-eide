- **Main decisions** (full list in `epics.md` → "Decision history"):
  - **File validation:** a PDF upload must pass the PDF content checks (the `%PDF-` signature and the PDF opening and page-count checks) and have a `.pdf` extension; a TXT upload must pass the text content checks (no known binary signature, no NUL bytes, strict UTF-8, no disallowed control characters) and have a `.txt` extension (case-insensitive). The extension alone never accepts a file.
  - **PDF signature:** `%PDF-` must be at the very start of the file, documented as a known limitation.
  - **Byte limit:** the counted file bytes decide the limit; a file of exactly 10,485,760 bytes is accepted.
  - **Privacy link:** opens in a new tab with the approved accessible name `Les mer om hvordan Anthropic behandler data, åpnes i ny fane`.
  - **Spending:** the app's limits are spending safeguards, not budget guarantees; there are no automatic retries; the README says a manual retry may make another paid call.
  - **Final demo fixture:** recorded by a developer-run script; candidate first, Git-ignored; never hand-edited; explicit approval before every paid attempt.
  - **Page-marker validation** is not semantic citation checking; citation quality is checked by hand.
  - **Reflection report:** the developer writes it personally; its exact requirements stay unresolved (U-2).
