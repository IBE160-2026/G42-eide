- **The three gates** (`backend/app/pipeline/stage5_gates.py`, 170 lines), run in the order Gate 1 → Gate 2 → Gate 3:
  - **Gate 1 (completeness):** any `stop` other than `complete` gives `ai_output_invalid`, before `raw_json` is read.
  - **Gate 2 (shape):**
    - **Parse:** `json.loads`, called through the module. Only `ValueError` and `RecursionError` are caught. The parsed value must be a JSON object.
    - **Status first:** exact string checks. `insufficient_content` stops the pass with that code without validating anything else. `ok` leads to `AiOutput.model_validate`, catching only `ValidationError`.
    - **Text:** the shared `is_valid_result_text` is applied to every text field.
  - **Gate 3 (counts and page-marker validation):**
    - **Counts first:** at least `MIN_ITEMS` flashcards, quiz items and key concepts.
    - **PDF:** pages are filtered to `supplied_pages`, then sorted and de-duplicated. Any flashcard, quiz item or key concept left with no pages fails the pass and is never dropped; the Summary may end up empty.
    - **Plain text:** any page, the Summary's included, fails.
    - **Unknown kind:** an unknown document kind raises `ValueError("unknown document kind")`.
    - **Build:** one guarded `try` builds `ValidatedResults`, catching only `ValidationError`.
- **The `ValidatedResults` contract** (`backend/app/contracts/results.py`, 95 lines):
  - `is_valid_result_text` (shared with Gate 2);
  - `ResultText`, a `StrictStr` with a fixed error message;
  - `ResultPages`, strict positive integers, strictly increasing, held in a tuple;
  - `ValidatedSummary`, `ValidatedFlashcard`, `ValidatedQuizItem`, `ValidatedKeyConcept` and `ValidatedResults`, all frozen, forbidding extra fields and with `hide_input_in_errors`.

  Collections are tuples internally and JSON arrays in JSON mode. The class docstring says direct construction proves only contract-level invariants.
- **Privacy safeguards:**
  - no logging or printing, and no calls to `errors()` or `json()`;
  - a caught exception only sets a flag and is never bound or stored;
  - a `PassFailure` is raised after the `except` clause, so `__context__` and `__cause__` are `None` and `__suppress_context__` is `False`;
  - a `PassFailure` holds only its code. That doesn't remove AI text from its traceback: the failure's frames still hold AI text in their locals (see Known limits).
