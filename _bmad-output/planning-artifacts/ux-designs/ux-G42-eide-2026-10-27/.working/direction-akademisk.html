Academic / editorial · accent #1B2A4E

An editorial, textbook-like direction: crisp white page, Georgia serif headings over a compact system-sans UI, and thin horizontal rules instead of boxes. The layout is denser (15px UI text, 2px corners) and hierarchy comes from type scale and weight rather than colour; a restrained navy carries interaction while burgundy is reserved for warnings and errors. It reads as serious and trustworthy, like a well-set reference book.
