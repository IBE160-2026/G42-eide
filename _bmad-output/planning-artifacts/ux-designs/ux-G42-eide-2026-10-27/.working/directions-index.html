AI Study Buddy – designretninger

Four visual personalities applied to the same hero screen (results, Flashkort tab) plus an error state and DEMO banner. Each file includes a rationale and a WCAG contrast table.

  • Rolig notatbok (Calm notebook, accent #1D5C63)

    Calm, warm paper, generous spacing and a deep-teal ink accent — a quiet, focused study session.

  • Akademisk (Academic / editorial, accent #1B2A4E)

    Scholarly textbook feel — Georgia serif headings, thin rules instead of boxes, dense layout, navy accent with burgundy for warnings/errors.

  • Vennlig (Friendly, accent #6B2BD9)

    Encouraging and approachable — saturated purple, big rounded cards with soft shadows, pill buttons and large touch targets.

  • Høykontrast (High-contrast utilitarian, accent #0037B3)

    No-nonsense, accessibility-first — black on white, 2px black borders, square corners, one strong blue accent and a thick black/yellow focus ring.
