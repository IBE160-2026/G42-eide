Høykontrast

High-contrast utilitarian · accent #0037B3

A utilitarian, accessibility-first direction: pure black text on white, 2px solid black borders on every control and region, square 0–2px corners and no decoration beyond what carries meaning. One strong functional blue marks interaction, status uses heavy-bordered blocks with icon tiles, and the focus ring is a thick 4px black outline with a yellow halo that cannot be missed. Type is a step larger and bolder for maximum legibility at a glance.
